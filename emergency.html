🚨 Emergency Lines
Every hero has a team, and these are yours!
These grown-ups are always awake and ready to keep you safe.
Please only call if there is a real emergency. This keeps the lines open so our Helpers can race to anyone who needs them right away.
💡 Not a big emergency? If you just want to talk about your feelings, tap the blue 'I want to talk' button on the home screen instead. We're always here for you!
🚔
Nigeria Police
Crime, danger & security emergencies
112
The Nigeria Police Force responds to crimes, threats, violence and security emergencies. Call them if you are being followed, attacked, or witness a crime. They are available 24 hours a day.
📞 Call 112 — Nigeria Police
🚑
Ambulance Service
Medical emergencies & injuries
115
Call for urgent medical help — if someone is injured, unconscious, or very sick. Trained paramedics will come to you with medical equipment and take the person to the nearest hospital.
📞 Call 115 — Ambulance
🚒
Fire Service
Fires & rescue operations
01-7944929
The Federal Fire Service puts out fires and rescues people from dangerous situations. If you see a fire — in a building, market, or vehicle — call them immediately and move away from the area.
📞 Call Fire Service
🛑
NAPTIP
Child trafficking & exploitation
0800-770-0800
NAPTIP (National Agency for the Prohibition of Trafficking in Persons) protects children and adults from being trafficked or exploited. If someone is trying to take you somewhere against your will, or offering you a "job" that feels wrong — call them. The hotline is free and available 24/7.
📞 Call NAPTIP Hotline
💜
Sexual Assault Helpline
Safe, confidential support
0800-033-3
Project Alert on Violence Against Women provides free, confidential help for survivors of sexual assault or abuse. You can call if something has happened to you, or if you are afraid. You will not be judged. They can connect you with a safe place, medical help, and legal support.
📞 Call Sexual Assault Helpline
Emergency Services — Kenya
🚔
Kenya Police
Crime, danger & security emergencies
999
The Kenya Police Service responds to crimes, threats, and violence. Call 999 if you are in immediate danger, being followed, or witnessing a crime. Available 24 hours a day nationwide.
📞 Call 999 — Kenya Police
🚑
Kenya Red Cross Ambulance
Medical emergencies & injuries
1199
Dial 1199 to reach the Kenya Red Cross ambulance service. It is free and available 24/7 nationwide. Call if someone is injured, unconscious, or in a medical emergency. Trained paramedics will respond.
📞 Call 1199 — Red Cross Ambulance
🚒
Nairobi Fire Service
Fires & rescue operations
020-2222181
The Nairobi City County Fire Service puts out fires and rescues people from dangerous buildings or situations. If you see a fire, move away from the area and call immediately. Do not try to fight a fire yourself.
📞 Call Nairobi Fire Service
🧒
Child Helpline Kenya
Child abuse, trafficking & exploitation
116
The Kenya Child Helpline (116) is a free, 24/7 line for children who are being abused, neglected, or exploited. If someone is hurting you, trafficking you, or you feel unsafe — call 116. Children who call are taken to safe houses run by the government.
📞 Call 116 — Child Helpline
💜
GBV & Sexual Assault Hotline
Safe, confidential support
1195
Kenya's national GBV hotline 1195 is free, confidential, and available 24/7. It supports survivors of sexual assault, domestic violence, and harmful practices like FGM. You can also send an SMS if you cannot speak. Counsellors can mobilise police and ambulances for you in emergencies.
📞 Call 1195 — GBV Hotline
